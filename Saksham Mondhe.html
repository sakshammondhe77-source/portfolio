Projects

Apartment Rental Manager - Nestora

Nestora was one of my projects designed to aid property rental inspections. It facilitates both landlords and renters to photograph properties and maintain a visual history of their condition. Furthermore, it enables users to analyse 'before' and 'after' images of a property and monitor any damage and repairs that may be required.

Jarvis - AI Personal Assistant

For this project I investigated how AI can be utilised to generate a personal assistant. By using Python I was able to test how to produce an assistant that could recognise and reply to user instructions. Undertaking this project enabled me to further understand the interplay between AI and programming.

AI Shopping Assistant

Given the multitude of online shopping options available, often it can be difficult for consumers to decide upon which product to purchase. This prompted me to formulate this concept. The objective is to design a Telegram based assistant which can provide customers with helpful product suggestions, so they do not have to spend extensive amounts of time perusing multiple websites for information.
